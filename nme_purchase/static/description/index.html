Nusantara ERP - Purchase

Requests for quotation and purchase orders with tiered approval.

Features

  • Approval above a configurable amount, with manager activities
  • Automatic receipts valued at the net purchase price
  • Received quantity from the stock ledger, vendor returns
  • RFQ / purchase order PDF and email

Part of Nusantara Mini ERP, a portfolio project on Odoo 20 Community by M. Fadli Kurniawan. Source code and documentation.
